5. SCIENTIFIC INVESTIGATION

BEYOND THE MYSTERY

The extraordinary circumstances of the discovery require extraordinary scrutiny.
            Specialists may examine:
Material Analysis
Determining the composition of pigments, binders, canvas, wood, or other materials.
Dating
Where appropriate, scientific dating techniques may help establish an 
            estimated age of suitable organic materials.
Pigment Analysis
Identifying pigments can provide clues about manufacturing techniques and
            historical periods.
Microscopic Examination
Studying layers, brushwork, deterioration patterns, and surface characteristics.
Environmental Analysis
Sediment and surrounding material may provide information about the object's 
            underwater history.
Provenance Research
Historical records, shipping archives, archaeological databases, and other
            documentation may help establish its journey.
Scientific examination is ongoing. No definitive historical attribution 
            should be made until supported by appropriate evidence.
